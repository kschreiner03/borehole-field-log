# MODFLOW 6

`resources/mf6/<platform>-<arch>/` holds the official MODFLOW 6 executable, version 6.8.1, from the U.S. Geological Survey
(https://github.com/MODFLOW-ORG/modflow6/releases/tag/6.8.1). MODFLOW 6 is public domain (CC0-1.0); see
https://code.usgs.gov/usgs/modflow/modflow6/blob/master/LICENSE.md and the USGS disclaimer at
https://code.usgs.gov/usgs/modflow/modflow6/blob/master/DISCLAIMER.md.

Fetch it with `node scripts/fetch-mf6.mjs` (checked against the SHA-256 published with the release).
Reference: Langevin, C.D., Hughes, J.D., Provost, A.M., Russcher, M.J., Banta, E.R., Niswonger, R.G., Panday, S., Merrick, D.,
Morway, E.D., and Bakker, M., MODFLOW 6 Modular Hydrologic Model, U.S. Geological Survey Software.
