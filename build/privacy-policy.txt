BOREHOLE FIELD LOG
PRIVACY POLICY AND TERMS OF USE

Effective 9 November 2026

Please read this before installing. Choosing "I Agree" means you accept it.


1. WHAT THE APP COLLECTS

Nothing. Borehole Field Log has no account or sign-in, no analytics or
usage tracking, no advertising, and sends no crash reports. It does not
check for updates or contact any server of ours. Nothing you enter is
sent to us. The only things sent anywhere are the map requests in
section 3.


2. WHERE YOUR DATA IS KEPT

Everything you enter stays on this computer:

- Boreholes, company settings and site plans, photos and imagery are kept
  in the app's profile folder:
  %APPDATA%\Borehole Field Log
- If you set up Apple Maps, your Maps key (Team ID, Key ID and key) is
  kept in the same folder, in apple-maps.json. It is never put in a
  project file.
- Project files (.bhp, .bhlog) and exports (PDF, Excel, AGS) are saved
  only where you choose to save them.
- Groundwater models are run by MODFLOW 6 on this computer, in a temporary
  folder that is deleted when the run finishes.

You control this data. Field records can include client, site and
landowner information; keep and share project files according to your
own organisation's privacy obligations.


3. WHEN THE APP USES THE INTERNET

The app works offline. It goes online only to draw a map background, and
only while you are viewing a map:

- Satellite, topographic and light grey maps come from Esri
  (server.arcgisonline.com).
- The street map comes from OpenStreetMap (tile.openstreetmap.org).
- Satellite (Apple Maps) is used only if you set it up with a Maps key
  from your own Apple Developer account. Pictures come from Apple
  (snapshot.apple-mapkit.com); each request carries the centre and zoom
  of the area in view and your Team ID and Key ID, signed on this
  computer. The key itself is never sent.

These requests tell that provider your computer's IP address and which
map area you are viewing. No borehole data, names or files are sent.
Map pieces you have viewed are kept on this computer so they show
offline. The providers' own privacy policies apply to those requests:
  https://www.esri.com/en-us/privacy/overview
  https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy
  https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

Web links in the app open in your own web browser.


4. UNINSTALLING

Uninstalling removes the app but keeps your saved data, so reinstalling
brings it back. To remove the data as well, delete the folder
%APPDATA%\Borehole Field Log after uninstalling. Project files you saved
elsewhere are never removed.
